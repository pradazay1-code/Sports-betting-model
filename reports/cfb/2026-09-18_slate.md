## Method note — what changed since last night

Last night's DET @ BUF total missed by 21 points because I ran two projection
forms, discarded the higher one as "absurd," and bet 2u on the lower. The higher
one was closer. So today **every total below is run through three methods** —
additive PPG, multiplicative PPG, and an opponent-adjusted yards model — and
**the spread between them is reported as the uncertainty rather than averaged
away.**

Where the methods converge, there is a number. Where they don't, I say so.

**One structural limit up front.** CollegeFootballData needs a key I don't have,
ESPN's API is blocked by the egress proxy, and I could retrieve SP+ **ranks** but
not **ratings**. Per the rule I just wrote into CLAUDE.md §3.4b: without a ratings
spine, **I do not publish spread numbers in this sport.** A PPG model run on
Miami-Wake Forest returns a pick'em against a market of -20.5 — that is the model
failing, not an edge. Totals survive; sides do not.
