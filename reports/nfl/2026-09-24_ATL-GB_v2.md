## 1. THE MECHANISM — the insight my first pass missed entirely

Last time I called London's receptions and Bijan's receptions "the same bet — Atlanta throws."
**They are not the same bet.**

> **A 45.6% pressure rate against a damaged line does not suppress completions. It suppresses
> yards per completion.** The ball comes out fast and short: checkdowns to the back, quick
> hitches to the X.

That single fact does three things at once:
- **Feeds** Bijan's reception volume (he is the pressure valve)
- **Kills** London's and Atlanta's yardage
- **Suppresses the game total** — drives that move via 5-yard checkdowns stall in field-goal range

**Bijan catching five passes for 30 yards and Atlanta failing to reach the end zone are the
same event.** Hold that thought; it is the parlay.

Note both tails hurt: a blowout kills the receptions leg, a shootout kills the under. **Build A
needs the middle — a competitive, ugly, low-scoring game.** That is my central projection, but
it is a projection.
