## 4. TOUCHDOWN SCORERS — and a board I refused to use

**I could not devig this board, and the reason matters.**

My first pass returned **every single player at +18% to +65% EV.** That is
impossible, and it was a modeling failure, not a gift. The eight names I retrieved
summed to **3.08** expected touchdowns against a **5.20**-touchdown game, so the
devig scaled everyone up 69% to fill the gap.

The gap was fake. **The list mixed anytime prices with first-touchdown prices.**
Two sources gave "a **69.0%** chance Walker scores" and "a **59.9%** chance Taylor
scores" — that is -223 and -149. The list I pulled had Taylor at **+245**, which is
29%. A price cannot be both. **+245 is his first-TD number.**

Same trap as Kincaid on Thursday (-175 in one place, +190 in another). A mixed board
cannot be devigged, and scaling one up produces exactly the kind of number that looks
like free money and isn't.
