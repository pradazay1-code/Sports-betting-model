### What FanDuel's "Your Way" actually does

**You pick the line. FanDuel prices it.** Moving Drake London from 5.5 receptions to
4.5 raises his hit probability from 48.7% to 67.0% — and the payout falls to match.
**You do not gain equity by choosing an easier number.** Custom and alternate lines
also typically carry a *wider* hold than the main line, not a narrower one.

The edge exists in exactly one place: **where their price for a given number
disagrees with reality.** That is what everything below is built on.

## 3. THE PARLAY

### The one real mispricing on this board

| | Receptions | Receiving yards |
|---|---|---|
| **Drake London with Penix — 14 career games `[FACT]`** | **6.5/game** | **92/game** |
| **Drake London with Cooper Rush — 2026 Weeks 1-2 `[FACT]`** | **6 TOTAL** | **80 TOTAL** |
| **Tonight's market line** | **5.5** | **66.5** |

The market has split the difference between a functioning Atlanta offense and the one
that scored 16 points in two games. **If Penix is Penix, the whole London curve is
priced low** — at the main line and at every custom line you can build off it.

**Bijan Robinson `[FACT]`:** line **4.5 receptions**; he hit 4.5+ in **3 of his last 5
games with Penix.** 4.2 yards per carry this season with defenses keying on him.

### The build — two legs, both Atlanta passing game

| Leg | "Your Way" line | Market line | P(hit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drake London 5+ receptions** | over 4.5 | 5.5 | **67.0%** |
| **Bijan Robinson 4+ receptions** | over 3.5 | 4.5 | **66.0%** |

**These legs are positively correlated** — both need the same thing, Penix functional
and Atlanta throwing. Independent product is 44.4% (+125). **With correlation the true
joint is 53.3%, fair -114.**

That correlation is also why FanDuel will price it *below* the independent product.
**The gap between their haircut and the real correlation is the entire edge.**

| Scenario | London | Bijan | **Joint** | Fair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BEAR** — Penix rusty, low volume | 54.4% | 52.8% | **38.7%** | +159 |
